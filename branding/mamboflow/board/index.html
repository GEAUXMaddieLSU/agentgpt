Pattern language

Funky backgrounds, strict rules.

Two of the four patterns come straight from Maddie's house: the foyer tile and the ceiling paper. Signal is the quiet default. Lattice is the paper's geometry with the color removed, for documents.

FoyerDark grounds

  • One per surfaceNever mix patterns. The panel counts as one.
  • PlacementBehind Mambo or beside a headline. Never behind body copy.
  • WeightSignal and Lattice in stone or hairline ink. Foyer at reduced opacity on ink. Argyle at full color, only inside the panel.
